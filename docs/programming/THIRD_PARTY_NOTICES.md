## Geometry and icons

The outdoor ants are original glTF 2.0 models, `koolApp/assets/models/ants/worker_black.glb` and `soldier.glb`, generated by `tools/make_black_ant_glb.py`. Their proportions follow a photograph of fire ants by Stephen Ausmus, USDA (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fire_ants_01.jpg, public domain). That photograph is not in this repository and is not used as a texture. The mesh is not a trace of the image. The outdoor prey, predator, spider, and ladybug are original models in `koolApp/assets/models/insects/`, generated by `tools/make_outdoor_insects_glb.py`. The ladybug's round shell follows a public-domain photograph from pdphoto.org (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ladybug.jpg, caption "Marienkäfer auf Blatt"). The file page does not name an author. That photograph is not in this repository and is not used as a texture. The mesh is not a trace of the image. The outdoor beetle, `koolApp/assets/models/insects/beetle.glb`, is an original stag-beetle silhouette generated by `tools/make_stag_beetle_glb.py` in Blender. Its proportions follow a photograph of a male *Lucanus cervus* by J.F. Gaffard, June 2004 (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lucanus_cervus.jpg, CC BY-SA 3.0 and GFDL). That photograph is not in this repository and is not used as a texture. The mesh is not a trace of the image. Sugar and protein morsels in `koolApp/assets/models/food/` are generated by `tools/make_food_glb.py`. The pebble, leaf, and clock stone in `koolApp/assets/models/nature/` are generated by `tools/make_props_glb.py`. The clover tuft and flower, `clover_tuft.glb` and `clover_flower.glb`, are generated by `tools/make_clover_glb.py`. Their proportions follow a photograph of white clover by Siddharth Patil (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trifolium_repens_macro.jpg, CC0). That photograph is not in this repository and is not used as a texture. The mesh is not a trace of the image. Since #69 the clover tuft has notched, heart-shaped leaflets in threes at 120 degrees and the flower is a low-poly floret crown; both are self-made by the project's Artist role, written by hand in `tools/make_clover_glb.py`, released under the project license, and carry their own `clover` / `clover_flower` materials. The headless renders in `docs/screenshots/clover-glb-headless-*.png` were made with Blender's `bpy` module (GPL, used only as an offline renderer; nothing from Blender ships in the app). The green aphid is likewise original. None of these are taken from Sketchfab or AntSim. Soil, rain, the nest cutaway, and the block fallbacks are procedural meshes in `com.neojou.myants.render3d`. A missing clover file falls back to those blocks.

HUD toolbar icons (Speed, Zoom, Tool rows) are Material Symbols Rounded SVGs (opsz 20, wght 400, FILL 0; `*_fill.svg` = FILL 1) by Google, Apache License 2.0, from https://github.com/google/material-design-icons (`symbols/web/<name>/materialsymbolsrounded/`). ## Fonts

English and German interface text, and the HUD digit slots, use kool's bundled Fira Sans MSDF.

Traditional Chinese and Japanese use an MSDF atlas generated from Noto Sans CJK TC (also known as Source Han Sans), weight 400. Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe. Reserved Font Names include "Source Han Sans" and "Noto Sans CJK". Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). The full font file is not in this repository. The atlas is `koolApp/src/commonMain/resources/fonts/noto-sans-cjk.json` and `noto-sans-cjk.png`, and it contains only glyphs the interface draws.
